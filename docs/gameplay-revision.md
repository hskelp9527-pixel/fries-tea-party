# A minute earned through curiosity

The latest user instruction prioritizes complete ordinary play. The default chapter keeps the garden, size changes, three size-specific gears, repair and closing song. Gear pickup requires the appropriate size and a reachable approach. The encounters below are optional, enabled only with `?challenges`; they never block the default chapter. Three mistakes permit a retry, and leaving retains earned progress.

| Place | Player action | Duration and failure |
|---|---|---|
| Tree hollow, giant | Move the hand with the mouse or arrows; hold over warm ticking brass and trace nine teeth | Nine 3.2-second holds plus searching; holding rough bark causes a slip |
| Fountain grotto, small | Choose one of three dry lanes and cross while the syrup parts | Nine openings at 3.8-second intervals; a wet or repeated step counts as a slip; each valid step moves the real character controller |
| Cottage mantel, normal | Compare recorded watch ticks, then open the watch with the calm double tick | Three rounds, at least three 3.6-second comparisons per round; wrong watches count as slips |

The initial objective names the three missing gears and shows 0/3. Glints and ticking suggest a direction. After 45, 80 and 115 seconds the Dormouse gives increasingly specific clues, each written as well as spoken so muting voice does not hide essential information. The objective and hint share a target; changing the active goal restarts its clue tier. Controls describe inputs without listing location solutions.

Large footsteps bounce the table cups. A giant can hold a drifting cup with G or the right mouse button. Small Fries feels the Dormouse's outward breath. These toys do not award gears.

Every chair runs at normal speed and remains seated until E or movement requests standing. Repeat tea uses a short real cup raise, sip and return; repeat cake uses a short bend with a visible magical morsel travelling from the cake to the actual lip landmark, then blends back to idle. A safety walk before growing can add movement time. No repeat branch accelerates the global simulation. Enter finishes a scripted scene through its authored end states; it cannot skip an optional gear challenge. Esc suspends movement, physics, tweens and audio together. Quality, interface/subtitle language and three volume controls are available in that menu.

Only lit rhythm windows score. Extra taps break the streak; final-chorus garden participation depends on accuracy. Results show elapsed play time, rhythm and discovered conversations, then allow a clean replay. Safe chapter checkpoints retain gears, puzzle results and discoveries.

`npm run test:browser` drives ordinary play through the real controller and checks recovery, pause, checkpoint reload, ending and replay. `node scripts/gameplay.mjs --no-skip` runs the natural main line and follows the visible rhythm cue; it does not claim human reaction testing. `--challenges` exercises the optional encounters. Camera steering is automated; those chapter regressions never set position or body size and never auto-jump over blocked routes.
