# A minute earned through curiosity

The latest user instruction prioritizes complete ordinary play. The default chapter keeps the garden, size changes, three size-specific gears, repair and closing song. Gear pickup requires the appropriate size and a reachable approach. Each gear is earned through a short challenge, listed below; `?nochallenges` turns them off and gives the gears directly. Three mistakes permit a retry, leaving retains earned progress, and Enter cannot skip a challenge.

| Place | Player action | Duration and failure |
|---|---|---|
| Tree hollow, giant | Move the hand with the mouse or arrows and find four brass teeth with no drawn target: the hand's glow warms and the ticking quickens smoothly as it nears a tooth; hold there | Four 1.6-second holds plus searching; holding rough bark causes a slip |
| Fountain grotto, small | Start where Fries stands and choose one of three lanes; the syrup thins on the safe lane 0.5 seconds before it parts, then cross | Five openings at 2.6-second intervals; a wet or repeated step counts as a slip; each valid step moves the real character controller; a retry walks Fries back to the start |
| Cottage mantel, normal | Listen to the recorded watch ticks (each tick also flashes the watch) and open any watch you have heard once, the one with the calm double tick | One round; the correct watch is random per attempt; a wrong watch counts as a slip |

The initial objective names the three missing gears and shows 0/3. Glints and ticking suggest a direction. After 45, 80 and 115 seconds the Dormouse gives increasingly specific clues, each written as well as spoken so muting voice does not hide essential information. The objective and hint share a target; changing the active goal restarts its clue tier. Controls describe inputs without listing location solutions.

Large footsteps bounce the table cups. A giant can hold a drifting cup with G or the right mouse button. Small Fries walks at 0.35 m/s and feels the Dormouse's outward breath; a small cake outside the grotto and a giant cake beside the tree gear shorten the return to normal size. The controls line lists the cup grab (G or right mouse) while big. These toys do not award gears.

Every chair runs at normal speed and remains seated until E or movement requests standing. Repeat tea uses a short real cup raise, sip and return; repeat cake uses a short bend with a visible magical morsel travelling from the cake to the actual lip landmark, then blends back to idle. A safety walk before growing can add movement time. No repeat branch accelerates the global simulation. Enter finishes a scripted scene through its authored end states; it cannot skip an optional gear challenge. Esc suspends movement, physics, tweens and audio together. Quality, interface/subtitle language and three volume controls are available in that menu.

Only rhythm windows score (plus or minus 0.12 seconds); the cup lights 0.15 seconds earlier so the beat can be anticipated, and a tap before the window does not score. Extra taps break the streak; final-chorus garden participation depends on accuracy. Results show elapsed play time, rhythm with a one-word grade, conversations found out of the total, each gear challenge's time and slips, then allow a clean replay. Safe chapter checkpoints retain gears, puzzle results and discoveries.

`npm run test:browser` drives ordinary play through the real controller and checks recovery, pause, checkpoint reload, ending and replay. `node scripts/gameplay.mjs --no-skip` runs the natural main line and follows the visible rhythm cue; it does not claim human reaction testing. The challenges are part of both routes. Camera steering is automated; those chapter regressions never set position or body size and never auto-jump over blocked routes.
