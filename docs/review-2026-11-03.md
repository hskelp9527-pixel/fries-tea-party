## The ten biggest gaps, by how much closing them would raise the score

1. **The sun never enters the frame** (about +1.2, code). Put a low sun visible between the tree's branches behind the main views, open light gaps in the canopy, cut the directionless fill, keep a soft warm bounce on faces, layer height fog (clear near, warm far), stop the lanterns blooming. Pass: the sun is visible, hair has a thin gold rim, shadow sides stay readable, cup rims and the table catch warm highlights.
2. **The big tree is a green ceiling** (about +1.0, model + code). A twisted trunk with spreading roots and arching main branches; foliage in clusters with gaps for light, leaves lit through at the edges; round brass clocks with hands at six and glass lanterns with a candle. Pass: the tree frames the upper left; in s14 no actor is hidden by leaves.
3. **The table is empty** (about +0.9, model + texture + layout). A patterned cloth with folds and a hanging hem, teapots, cake stands, candles, spoons, jam, books and sweets in three clusters (in front of each host, a centrepiece, foreground overlap), leaving room for hands. Pass: in s03/s13 at least two thirds of the visible tabletop is covered.
4. **The garden is a fenced flat lawn** (about +0.7, layout + models). See "Map" below.
5. **The two landmarks have the wrong silhouettes** (about +0.5, models). A round teaware fountain, and a thatched cottage with rabbit-ear chimneys, round windows and a glowing porch. Pass: both are recognisable in black and white silhouette.
6. **The magic reads as debris** (about +0.4, code). Curved translucent petals, butterflies with real wings, soft steam, tea and syrup as continuous glossy streams with fine drops, a little gold trail on gears and the page only.
7. **The characters don't act the story** (about +0.35, animation). Holding up the watch, the burst of gears and everyone's reaction, Fries showing a gear, hands mending the watch together, stuffing the Dormouse into the teapot. Props bound to the hand bones; gaze fixed on the right target.
8. **The small-scale places and the ending are not places** (about +0.3). Under the table: carved legs, the cloth's fringe, crumbs, mushrooms; inside the fountain's grotto: an arched stone mouth, wet floor, moss, hanging syrup; behind the tree door: a deep path of red roses ending in a heart-shaped arch.
9. **Big is not giant** (about +0.25, layout + camera). The giant's eye line must clear the cottage's eaves and see the whole table; put two or three floating cups and spoons near the giant's camera for scale.
10. **The cameras don't direct attention** (about +0.2, camera). The opening looks over Fries's shoulder at the hosts; mending the watch cuts to the hands; the song starts close and rises without going behind leaves; the ending keeps the door on the left and the teapot business on the right. Depth of field only in close-ups.
